IMCI fever

A form for a sick child with fever, derived from the WHO digital adaptation kit for child health (2024): it asks the questions, classifies the case and lists the treatments, each with the WHO rules it comes from.

Pension money for a home

The early withdrawal of Swiss occupational pension money for a home (BVG Art. 30a–30g, WEFV), as a step-by-step application in English, German, French and Italian. Its rules are SPARQL queries in the knowledge model, with the dates of the law: it says whether the withdrawal is possible, the maximum amount, the deadlines and the documents to attach, each with its article. Everything runs in your browser.
